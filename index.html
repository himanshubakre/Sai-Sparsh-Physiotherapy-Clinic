About the Clinic

A dedicated physiotherapy & rehabilitation clinic in Bhayandar East

Sai Sparsh Physiotherapy Clinic is a local physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic supporting patients with pain management, mobility limitations and rehabilitation needs. We work with people recovering from injury, managing musculoskeletal conditions, or looking to improve movement and function in everyday life.

Located on Goddev Naka Road, opposite Ashwini Hospital, the clinic is easy to reach for patients across Bhayandar East and the wider Mira Bhayandar area, offering a focused, patient-centred approach to physiotherapy care.

What We Treat

Physiotherapy Services

Focused treatment programmes for pain, injury and mobility concerns.

Sports Physiotherapy

Assessment and rehabilitation support for sports-related injuries and performance-limiting movement issues.

Back & Neck Pain

Structured physiotherapy for back and neck pain, including cervical and lumbar rehabilitation programmes.

Frozen Shoulder Rehabilitation

Targeted mobility and rehabilitation exercises to help manage frozen shoulder and restricted movement.

Neurological Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy support tailored to neurological conditions, focused on movement, function and independence.

Injury Rehabilitation

Guided recovery programmes to help restore strength, mobility and function following an injury.

Exercise & Mobility Programs

Personalised, exercise-based rehabilitation programmes designed to build strength and improve mobility.
